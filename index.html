Nirmal Setu

Privacy Policy | Last Updated: November 25, 2026

Overview: Nirmal Setu is an enterprise operations platform developed and operated by Urmila International Services Pvt. Ltd. ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") to facilitate On-Board Housekeeping Services (OBHS), coach sanitation, staff attendance, and quality inspections for Indian Railways contracts. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, and protect your information when using the Nirmal Setu mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

To deliver authorized railway housekeeping operations, attendance verification, and operational reporting, the application collects the following categories of data:

A. Precise & Approximate Geolocation

  • Data Collected: GPS coordinates, latitude, longitude, and station geofence proximity.
  • Purpose: Geofencing verification to ensure staff and supervisors are physically present at assigned railway stations, yards, or running trains when marking live attendance or submitting coach audit reports.
  • Scope: Location is accessed only while the app is in foreground use. We do not track location in the background when the app is closed.

B. Camera & Photographs

  • Data Collected: Photos captured via the in-app camera.
  • Purpose: Live selfie attendance verification, photographic evidence of coach cleanliness audits, toilet inspection proof, and task completion verification.
  • Scope: The camera is triggered solely when initiated by the user for operational records.

C. Device Identifiers & Push Notifications

  • Data Collected: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device registration tokens, device model, operating system version.
  • Purpose: Delivering real-time operational alerts, coach duty assignments, and urgent supervisor notices.

D. Biometric Authentication Data

  • Data Processed: Fingerprint and Face/PIN verification via native Android BiometricPrompt and iOS LocalAuthentication.
  • Privacy Protection: We never collect, store, or transmit your raw biometric data. All biometric verification happens strictly on-device through hardware-level secure enclaves. The app receives only a cryptographic boolean confirmation (success/failure) from the device operating system.

2. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used strictly for enterprise operations, including:

  • Authenticating authorized contractor employees, supervisors, and administrative personnel.
  • Verifying duty attendance and punctual execution of OBHS cleaning shifts.
  • Submitting audit reports, coach inspection scores, and photo proofs for quality records.
  • Facilitating document downloads (e.g., duty passes, inspection PDFs).
  • Ensuring application stability and preventing unauthorized access.

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We respect your privacy and enforce strict data confidentiality:

  • No Commercial Sale: We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal or operational data to third parties or advertising networks.
  • Authorized Service Providers: Operational records may be securely transmitted to encrypted enterprise cloud infrastructure (e.g., Microsoft Azure / authorized servers) hosted by Urmila International Services Pvt. Ltd.
  • Regulatory & Contractual Obligations: Performance and attendance logs may be reviewed by authorized railway contract authorities as required under contractual obligations for OBHS operations.

4. Data Security & Encryption

We enforce robust enterprise security measures:

  • Encrypted in Transit: All data transmitted between the mobile application and our servers is strictly encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS 1.3 protocols.
  • No Cleartext Traffic: Unencrypted HTTP communication is blocked in production releases.
  • Secure Key Storage: Authentication tokens are stored securely in the device's encrypted storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

  • Retention: Operational and attendance records are retained in compliance with statutory labor laws and contractual record-keeping obligations.
  • Account & Data Deletion: If you are an employee or contractor seeking account deactivation or deletion of your operational data upon tenure completion, you may request data deletion by contacting your designated operations manager or emailing our support desk at App_Support@uistech.in.

6. Children's Privacy

Nirmal Setu is an enterprise workforce management tool intended solely for individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect operational or regulatory changes. Updated versions will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
